How to Calculate Percentage?

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.

Formula:

Percentage Result = (Number × Percentage) ÷ 100

For example, 20% of 500 is:

(500 × 20) ÷ 100 = 100
