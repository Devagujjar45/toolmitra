About Us

Welcome to Tool Mitra, a simple and free online tools website designed to make everyday calculations easier.

Tool Mitra provides useful tools such as percentage, age, BMI, GST, EMI, discount, unit conversion and calculator tools.

Our goal is to provide simple, easy-to-use and mobile-friendly tools that anyone can access without complicated software.

Our Mission

We aim to make useful online tools easily accessible to everyone. We continuously work on improving our tools and adding new features.
